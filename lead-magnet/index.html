Build from evidence.
Not assumptions.

Starting a product business often creates pressure to move quickly: source the product, prepare packaging, open the store and start promoting. But the earliest decisions are also the ones that can be expensive to reverse.

This chapter introduces the thinking behind a five-gate approach: understand the market, make buyer assumptions visible, identify what needs testing, set a meaningful threshold and decide what to do next.

01 Define the decision you need evidence to support.02 Separate market signals from assumptions.03 Think about what would validate—or challenge—your plan.04 Make the next move clearer before committing more resources.
