# Market Mind — Brand Identity V2 (2026)

## Brand idea
Data tells us what is happening. Insight tells us why. Strategy decides what happens next.

Market Mind is a strategy-led marketing agency. We understand the market and the people in it, decide where to compete, then execute and measure.

## Palette
- Dark Navy: #1E2A38
- Warm Cream: #F6F1EA
- Terracotta: #C86E5A
- Light Peach: #EBA894

Dark Navy and Warm Cream should make up about 85% of a surface. Terracotta stays under 5% and is used as a precise line, number or single call to action.

## Typography
- Display: Montserrat / Proxima Nova Bold. Headlines and numbers only, from 32px upward, tracked +2% to +6%; never for full sentences.
- Text: Montserrat. Light for statements, Regular for reading, Semibold for emphasis, Extrabold Italic for the one line that must be remembered.

## Graphic language
The Decision Line: one vertical line, always Terracotta, always alone. Evidence and context sit on one side; the choice or action on the other.

## Logo
Use the supplied full lockup (`assets/Market Mind New Logo.jpg`) for the website header, footer and primary brand applications. Use the standalone icon (`assets/market-mind-mark.webp`) only in restricted spaces such as social avatars, favicons and app icons. Keep clear space equal to the height of the M in the wordmark on all sides. Never stretch, squash, rotate, recolour, shadow, rebuild or place the mark on busy imagery. Minimum icon size: 24px on screen and 8mm in print.

## Voice
Speak like a senior strategist. Prefer clear, commercially grounded language over generic claims such as "360 solutions" or "revolutionary ROI". Explain what the work helps a business decide and how progress is measured.

## Brand promise
UNDERSTAND BEFORE YOU MOVE.
