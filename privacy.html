MARKET MIND / SITE INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

This notice explains how information submitted through website enquiries is handled and how links to third-party resources work.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Who is responsible?

Market Mind is an independent strategic marketing consultancy led by Manel Krichene. For privacy questions or requests concerning information submitted through this website, contact krichenem@gmail.com.

Contact enquiries

When you submit the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, company or project, enquiry topic and message. The form is processed by Formspree and used to receive and respond to your enquiry. Do not include sensitive personal information in a general enquiry.

Free chapter requests

If you request the free chapter, the website form collects your name and email address, plus an optional company or project name. Formspree processes the submission so Market Mind can record and manage the request. After a successful submission, the page reveals a link to claim the chapter through Gumroad, which handles the download under its own terms and privacy practices. An optional, unchecked marketing-consent box lets you request occasional Market Mind resources and field notes by email. You can request the chapter without opting into marketing messages and withdraw marketing consent by contacting krichenem@gmail.com.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, understand the context of a potential project, provide a requested resource, and send occasional marketing updates only where you have opted in. Market Mind does not sell form-submission information. Submissions are stored and processed by the third-party provider under its own terms and privacy practices. You can ask Market Mind to correct or delete information held through its workflow.

Third-party services

Formspree processes website contact and free-chapter request forms. Calendly is linked for booking discovery calls; when you open Calendly, its own privacy terms apply. Gumroad handles the free chapter claim and download experience, and processes any paid digital product checkout and delivery under its own terms. LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook links open their respective platforms. Market Mind does not embed social feeds on this page.

Cookies, analytics and hosting

This website does not currently include a configured analytics or advertising pixel. Cloudflare hosting may process technical request data to deliver and protect the website. If analytics, advertising, embedded scheduling, newsletter automation or other tracking technologies are added, this notice should be updated to describe them before activation.

Retention and security

Form data is handled by the third-party service and retained according to its service settings and applicable terms. Market Mind takes reasonable care when handling enquiry information but cannot guarantee the security practices or availability of a third-party platform.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit a form, opt out of marketing messages, or contact Market Mind to request access, correction or deletion of information where applicable. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights may apply.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the site and its integrations change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

This notice is a practical starting point and is not legal advice. Review it against actual Formspree settings, retention practices and the laws applicable to your customers.
