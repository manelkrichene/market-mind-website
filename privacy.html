Privacy, in plain language.

A simple explanation of how this website currently handles enquiry information.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Contact form

The current contact form prepares an email in your device's default email application. When you send it, the message is delivered to Market Mind at krichenem@gmail.com. The website itself does not currently store form submissions.

Analytics and cookies

This version does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information as part of serving the site. If analytics, scheduling, forms or third-party services are connected, this notice must be revised to describe the actual data flows.

External services

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, email, and future checkout or scheduling providers take you to services operated by third parties. Their own privacy terms apply when you use them.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to krichenem@gmail.com.

This is a practical website notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed if Market Mind adds analytics, newsletter subscriptions, embedded scheduling, a hosted form provider or e-commerce checkout.
