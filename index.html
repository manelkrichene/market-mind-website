Market Mind is led by Manel Krichene, a marketing researcher and strategist. The work combines consumer understanding, market evidence and commercial thinking to help teams make better-informed decisions.

The approach is practical: ask the right question, use relevant evidence, make assumptions visible and recommend a clear next step. The aim is not a report for its own sake. It is a decision the business can act on.
